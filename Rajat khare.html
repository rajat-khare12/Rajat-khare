Who is Rajat Khare?

Rajat Khare is a venture capitalist born in India and the founder of Boundary Holding, a European investment firm that focuses on deep-tech startups. He is known for his long-range view of technology and has become a visible name in innovation spanning artificial intelligence, robotics, aerospace, cybersecurity and medical technology.

Working mainly from Luxembourg, Khare has put together an international network of investments in young companies built on disruptive technology. His analytical, scale-minded approach has made him a recognised voice in venture capital.

Boundary Holding: the heart of his investment network

Boundary Holding is a modern investment firm that finds and supports early-stage and growth-stage companies with strong technological potential. With its base in Europe, it helps carry advanced research out of the lab and into global markets.

Focus sectors:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Aerospace & Drones
  • Cybersecurity & Surveillance
  • HealthTech & MedTech
  • Robotics & Automation

Under Khare, the firm has backed more than two dozen companies in Europe, Israel and Asia.

Vision and philosophy

Khare looks for technologies that solve real problems while staying ethical and ready for the future. His priorities:

  • Adopting deep technologies early
  • Scaling across borders
  • Regulatory readiness and compliance
  • Responsible use of AI and automation

His recent interests include agentic AI, green technology and autonomous systems.

Recognition and influence

Khare’s work has been covered by international business and technology media. He has been invited to policy discussions and panels on the future of deep-tech investment in Europe and Asia, and his career is also featured in a case study at INSEAD Business School.

In short: through Boundary Holding, Rajat Khare continues to shape global technology by investing in deep-tech companies aimed at a more connected, secure and innovative future.
